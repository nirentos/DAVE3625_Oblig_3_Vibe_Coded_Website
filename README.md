The Local Frequency Player is an offline music player that lets you organize and enjoy music directly from your computer. It features automatic BPM detection, customizable playlists, BPM-based sorting and filtering, adjustable playback speed, and a built-in equalizer. You can also search for tracks, save favorites, and manually fine-tune BPM values. Built as a single HTML file, Local Frequency runs directly in your browser without installation, accounts, or an internet connection.
I had this made to have a more usable music player for my files than just the local windows player or manually adding files to spotify.
